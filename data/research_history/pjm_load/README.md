# `data/research_history/pjm_load/`

The frozen research record behind PJM load. Three subfolders:

- [`scripts/`](scripts/README.md) — the eleven weekly research scripts, from `eda_exploration.py` through `week7_calibrate.py` and `zone_expansion.py`.
- [`reports/`](reports/README.md) — the reports and figures those scripts produced (Week 5 benchmark pre- and post-fix, Week 6 architecture/noise/conditional studies, Week 7 results, Phase 2 zone study).
- [`outputs/`](outputs/README.md) — the datasets and checkpoints they produced: `week3_outputs/` and `zone_outputs/<ZONE>/` for all 17 researched zones.

The provenance table mapping each script to its status and to what superseded it is in the [category README](../README.md#provenance-table). Wind has no counterpart here — see [`../wind/README.md`](../wind/README.md).
